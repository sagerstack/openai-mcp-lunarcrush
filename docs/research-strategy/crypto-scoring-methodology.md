# V7.0 Comprehensive Cryptocurrency Research Methodology

## Overview

V7.0 represents a comprehensive research framework that balances social sentiment, price dynamics, macro environment, and fundamental analysis to identify cryptocurrency investment opportunities with strong potential for appreciation.

## Philosophy

Our approach is based on **comprehensive opportunity identification** without portfolio management. We focus on systematic research using a multi-factor scoring system that evaluates candidates across five critical dimensions:

1. **Social Momentum** - Community engagement and viral potential
2. **Price Dynamics** - Market action and volume patterns
3. **Macro Environment** - Regulatory and economic conditions
4. **Fundamentals** - Technology, team, and business model strength
5. **Catalyst Potential** - Timeline and probability of price-moving events

## Entry Criteria

### **Minimum Thresholds**
- **Overall Score:** 70+ points for research recommendation
- **Social Momentum:** Minimum 15/30 points
- **Macro Environment:** No negative scoring (<4 points)
- **Fundamentals:** Minimum 15/25 points

### **Disqualification Factors**
- **Parabolic Risk:** >50% gain in 30 days = automatic disqualification
- **Distribution Pattern:** High volume with declining price
- **Negative Macro:** Score <4 in macro environment category
- **Fundamental Issues:** Score <10 in fundamentals category
- **Scam/Red Flags:** Any security or credibility concerns

### **Dynamic Sector Discovery Methodology**

**Core Sector Collection Framework (6 Fixed Sectors):**

1. **AI Sector** - Top 50 candidates by Galaxy Score
   - **Primary Metric**: Galaxy Score (overall health + momentum)
   - **Minimum Threshold**: Galaxy Score > 70
   - **Focus**: Artificial intelligence integration and automation

2. **DeFi Sector** - Top 25 candidates by market relevance
   - **Primary Metrics**: Market cap, TVL, protocol activity
   - **Minimum Threshold**: Market cap > $10M
   - **Focus**: Decentralized finance utility and adoption

3. **Privacy Sector** - All candidates with social momentum
   - **Primary Metrics**: Sentiment > 75%, social dominance > 0.1%
   - **Inclusive Approach**: No upper limit on candidates
   - **Focus**: Privacy enhancement and regulatory clarity

4. **Layer-1 Sector** - Top 30 candidates by development activity
   - **Primary Metrics**: Developer activity, transaction volume, ecosystem growth
   - **Minimum Threshold**: Daily transaction volume > 100K
   - **Focus**: Ecosystem competition and technological advancement

5. **Meme Sector** - Top 20 candidates by social engagement
   - **Primary Metrics**: Social dominance, community growth, virality
   - **Minimum Threshold**: Social dominance > 0.05%
   - **Focus**: Community-driven narratives and cultural impact

6. **New Launches Sector** - Top 30 recent launches
   - **Time Filter**: Tokens launched within last 30-90 days
   - **Primary Metrics**: Initial momentum, development activity, listing exchanges
   - **Focus**: Early opportunity identification and fresh project discovery

**Dynamic Discovery Process:**

1. **Comprehensive Trending Analysis**
   - Extract top 200 trending tokens from overall market
   - Remove tokens already captured in core sectors
   - Analyze remaining tokens for thematic clustering

2. **Pattern Recognition and Classification**
   - **Gaming/Metaverse Tokens**: Projects focused on gaming, NFTs, virtual worlds
   - **Real World Assets (RWA)**: Tokenized real estate, commodities, financial instruments
   - **Layer-2/Scaling Solutions**: Projects improving blockchain scalability and throughput
   - **AI Agents Tokens**: AI automation, agent networks, autonomous systems
   - **DePIN Infrastructure**: Decentralized physical infrastructure networks
   - **Exchange Tokens**: Trading platforms and market infrastructure
   - **Oracle Tokens**: Data feeds and information networks

3. **Adaptive Sector Creation**
   - **Minimum Threshold**: 10+ tokens showing similar characteristics
   - **Social Momentum**: Collective engagement metrics exceeding market baseline
   - **Market Relevance**: Sufficient trading volume and market cap presence
   - **Narrative Coherence**: Clear thematic relationship between tokens

4. **Quality Control and Validation**
   - Cross-reference tokens across multiple data sources
   - Verify credibility of social signals and market metrics
   - Check for manipulation or artificial engagement patterns
   - Ensure minimum data completeness standards
